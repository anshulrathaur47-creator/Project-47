# Fonts

All from Google Fonts (github.com/google/fonts), SIL Open Font License 1.1, free for commercial use.

- `PlayfairDisplay-Italic-VF.ttf`: Playfair Display Italic (variable weight 400–900), by Claus Eggers Sørensen. Key words in the ui-explainer style.
- `Poppins-Medium.ttf`, `Poppins-SemiBold.ttf`, `Poppins-Bold.ttf`: Poppins, by Indian Type Foundry. Captions and UI.
- `JetBrainsMono-VF.ttf`: JetBrains Mono (variable). Terminal cards.
