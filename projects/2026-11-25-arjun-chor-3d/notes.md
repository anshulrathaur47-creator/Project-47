# "रात आया मेरे घर एक चोर", v2 story: Arjun (user: "isme maza nahi aaya, ek adult boy ke upar banao")

**Idea:**
- Arjun, 24, came from a village to the city. He is awake at night in a PG room, reading rejection mails.
- A masked thief climbs in: "दे दे मुझे जो भी तेरा है". Arjun laughs, breaks, opens his arms: take it all.
- Each "… भी ले जा" line is a 2 s memory flashback (warm sepia). At the end of the 2 s the dark pulls it away (zoom, blur, darken).

  | Line | Memory |
  |---|---|
  | जीत | college stage, trophy up, confetti, crowd |
  | हार | walking out of an interview, a "REJECTED" letter flying |
  | डर | shivering alone in the rain under a street lamp, lightning |
  | घर | leaving home: Maa waving at the bus, a tear |
  | ख़्वाब | guitar on the rooftop, a dream bubble of a stage |
  | राज़ | a love letter he never gave; she reads on a bench |
  | ग़म | Papa's garlanded photo and a diya |
  | ज़ख़्म | she walks away with someone else; his heart cracks |
  | जात | boys laughing and pointing |
  | औक़ात | café: friends pay, his wallet is empty (a moth flutters out) |
  | बात | phone: typing "Maa, main theek hoon" to Maa, then deleting it (HTML phone UI) |
  | हालात | alone on a rooftop edge over the city, crane out |

- **Twist:** the thief pulls off his mask and hood, and it is Arjun himself, his own pain. Then a warm sunrise at the window, and Arjun smiles.
- **New character:** Arjun, short swept hair, a maroon hoodie, black joggers and white sneakers.
- **Build:** `src/Arjun3D.tsx`. Each memory is a small set at x = 40 + 14·i with its own camera.
